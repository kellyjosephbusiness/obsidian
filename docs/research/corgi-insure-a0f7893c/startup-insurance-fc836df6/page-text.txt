FAQ
What types of insurance does a startup need?
keyboard_arrow_down
Most startups need Directors & Officers (D&O) insurance for leadership protection, Errors & Omissions (E&O) for professional liability, Cyber Liability for data breach coverage, Employment Practices Liability (EPL), and General Liability (GL) for physical injury and property damage claims.
How much does startup insurance cost?
keyboard_arrow_down
Pre-seed packages are typically more affordable than growth-stage programs. Pre-seed startups typically pay $2,000–$5,000 per year for basic coverage. Cost depends on stage, industry, funding, headcount, and the coverages you select; however Series A companies average $5,000–$15,000 annually. See the full cost-by-stage breakdown. Run the instant quote for actual pricing, no sales call required.
When should a startup get insurance?
keyboard_arrow_down
Get insurance as soon as you incorporate or raise your first round. Most investors require D&O insurance before closing a funding round. Waiting until you need insurance means you may not be covered for prior acts. Getting coverage early with Corgi protects you from day one.
Do I need insurance before my funding round?
keyboard_arrow_down
Yes. Nearly all institutional investors require D&O insurance as a condition of investment. VCs and board members want personal liability protection before they join your cap table. Having coverage in place before fundraising signals professionalism and preparedness. Corgi binds D&O the same day.

Is Corgi's insurance financially secure? Is your carrier AM Best rated?
keyboard_arrow_down
Coverage is underwritten by Technology Risk Retention Group (TRRG), a federally chartered risk retention group regulated under the Liability Risk Retention Act (15 U.S.C. §3901). TRRG operates under federal prudential oversight and maintains dedicated reserves for policyholder obligations. Most risk retention groups are not rated by AM Best. AM Best does rate risk retention groups, and a number of RRGs carry AM Best's ratings — but roughly four in five RRGs operating in the United States, including TRRG, do not. An AM Best's Credit Rating is a voluntary, company-initiated engagement, and the absence of one is not a measure of financial condition. Coverage can also be placed through affiliated admitted carriers rated A- (Excellent) or better by AM Best, depending on state, line, and risk profile. Corgi Insurance Services, Inc. is a licensed producer in all states where it operates (CA License #6012791).
